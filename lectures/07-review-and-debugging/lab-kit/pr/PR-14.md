## Summary

This PR adds a **waitlist** for full events and turns booking confirmations into **real
email messages**.

### Waitlist

- `POST /api/events/{id}/waitlist` puts you in line for a full event (409 if the event
  still has places).
- When someone cancels, the first person waiting gets the freed place immediately, in the
  same transaction as the cancellation, and receives a notice.
- The programme now shows how many people are waiting for each event.
- A full event shows **Join waitlist** instead of a disabled button.
- Design notes are in `docs/waitlist.md`.

### Email confirmations

- Confirmations and promotion notices are now standard email messages (From, To and
  Subject headers), ready for the mail relay.
- **More robust:** confirmations are queued after the booking commits, so a problem in the
  notification step can never block or roll back a booking.

### Housekeeping

- Settings are collected in `app/settings.py`; each can be overridden by an environment
  variable.
- Replaced the slow end-to-end confirmation tests with fast, focused unit tests
  (`tests/test_notify.py`). The suite grew from 17 to 26 tests.
