# NNNN. A short noun phrase naming the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by:** the named person who owns this decision (and who else was involved)

## Context

The forces at play, stated as facts: the requirement, the constraints, what the code
looks like today, and what is likely to change. Neutral tone; no decision yet.

## Decision

"We will ...", in full sentences. Include the details a reader could get wrong, such as
units, time zones, and whether a boundary value is included.

## Alternatives considered

At least one option you rejected, and why. A rejected option you would revisit later
says when.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what you now have to watch. List the
negative and neutral consequences too, not only the positive ones.
